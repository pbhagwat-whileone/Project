# Existing Client

**Goal:**
Reach out to a client we are actively working with to propose an expansion, a new initiative, or relevant insight.

**Tone:**
Direct, familiar, and highly professional.

**Instructions:**
- Skip formal introductions. You already know them.
- Be direct about the reason for the email.
- Reference your active engagement or current context warmly.
- Keep the focus on adding value to what you are already doing together.
- Use a collaborative CTA.

**Structure:**
1. Warm, direct opening
2. The insight or proposal
3. How it ties into current work
4. Collaborative CTA
