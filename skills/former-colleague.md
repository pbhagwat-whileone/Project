# Former Colleague

**Goal:**
Reach out to someone you used to work with to catch up and explore potential synergies.

**Tone:**
Familiar, friendly, and peer-to-peer.

**Instructions:**
- Adopt a familiar, peer-to-peer tone.
- Acknowledge your shared history.
- Ask how things are going at their new role/company.
- Subtly introduce what you are doing now and how it might be relevant.

**Structure:**
1. Friendly greeting referencing past work
2. Check-in on their current role
3. Brief update on your current work
4. Catch-up CTA
