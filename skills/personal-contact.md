# Personal Contact

**Goal:**
Reach out to someone you have a personal relationship with to discuss business casually.

**Tone:**
Highly personal, friendly, and casual.

**Instructions:**
- Be highly personal, friendly, and casual.
- Skip formal company introductions.
- Focus on the personal connection first.
- Naturally transition to the business topic.

**Structure:**
1. Warm, personal greeting
2. Casual catch-up
3. Transition to the business topic
4. Casual, flexible CTA
