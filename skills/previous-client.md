# Previous Client

**Goal:**
Re-engage a past client by highlighting new capabilities or relevant insights.

**Tone:**
Warm, familiar, and professional.

**Instructions:**
- Re-establish connection warmly.
- Remind them of your past successes together if appropriate.
- Introduce the new reason for reaching out.
- Keep it light and focused on catching up or exploring a specific new angle.

**Structure:**
1. Warm re-engagement
2. Brief callback to past work
3. New insight/capability
4. Catch-up CTA
