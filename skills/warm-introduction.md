# Warm Introduction

## Purpose
Strongest trust level. Respond to an inbound inquiry or a recent direct introduction where both parties have opted in to the conversation.

## Tone & Familiarity
Welcoming, context-aware, and highly professional. The prospect expects this email, so skip any cold outreach framing.

## Skill-Specific Strategy

**Observation & Opening:**
- The introduction provides credibility. Use it once. Then focus on current business relevance.
- Do not waste email real estate explaining the introduction repeatedly.
- Example: "Great to connect. I understand [Company] is evaluating new orchestration frameworks..."

**Implication & Insight (Challenge):**
- Introduce a specific challenge tied to the observation to show you immediately grasp their situation.
- Example: "...and I know the transition can create significant performance overhead."

**Project Experience (Proof):**
- Provide ONE strong proof point as the centerpiece of the email.
- This should directly address the topic of the introduction.

**Conversational Question (CTA):**
- The CTA should propose clear next steps.
- Examples: "Let's schedule 30 minutes next week to discuss this further.", "Are you free Thursday afternoon for a brief introductory call?"
