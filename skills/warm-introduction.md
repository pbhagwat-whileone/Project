# Warm Introduction

**Goal:**
Establish a connection based on a mutual contact or shared context and express excitement to collaborate.

**Tone:**
Friendly, professional, and enthusiastic.

**Instructions:**
- Mention the mutual connection or the context of the introduction immediately in the first sentence.
- Express excitement to connect.
- Briefly highlight why the connection is mutually beneficial based on relevant expertise.
- Keep the call to action very low-friction.

**Structure:**
1. Mention the mutual connection/intro immediately
2. Express excitement
3. Brief relevant context
4. Low-friction CTA
