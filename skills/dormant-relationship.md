# Dormant Relationship

## Purpose
Use this skill when attempting to reconnect after a significant gap in communication, rekindling a previous relationship naturally.

## Relationship Context
We interacted in the past, but there has been a significant gap in time (e.g., > 6-12 months). There is history, but no immediate or active momentum.

## Outreach Objective
Reconnect naturally without being overly aggressive. Acknowledge the time gap gracefully and re-establish relevance for future discussions.

## Tone
Friendly, professional, patient. Do not sound demanding or urgent.

## Introduction Strategy
The opening sentence must explicitly but naturally acknowledge the time since the last interaction (e.g., "It's been a while since we last spoke about..."). Analyze the previous conversation history and reference a specific detail from it to prove genuine continuity.

## Company Context Usage
Use the recent company context (Tavily) as the specific, organic reason for reaching out *now*. Pivot smoothly from the past conversation into their recent company developments.

## WhileOne Positioning
Weave WhileOne capabilities in lightly (low/medium prominence). Focus strictly on how they align with their current/new initiatives rather than giving a broad overview or forcing a heavy capability list. Position WhileOne as a resource that naturally aligns with their new direction.

## Call To Action
The CTA should be very low-pressure (e.g., "Would love to catch up when you have a moment," or "Happy to hear how things have evolved on your end").

## Do
- Keep the email brief, conversational, and focused on relationship rekindling.
- Show genuine interest in how their business has evolved since you last spoke.

## Avoid
- Do NOT jump straight into a hard sales pitch.
- Avoid making assumptions about their current needs.
- Avoid sending a massive list of what WhileOne has been doing during the gap.
