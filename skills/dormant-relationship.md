# Dormant Relationship

## Purpose
Known relationship but inactive.

## Tone & Familiarity
Warm but professional. One brief acknowledgment of time gap. Do not spend multiple sentences discussing the gap. Immediately pivot into relevance.

## Skill-Specific Strategy

**Observation & Opening:**
- Acknowledge the gap naturally but briefly, then lead with ONE relevant observation about their current context.
- Example: "It's been some time since we last connected. I noticed your team's recent shift toward optimizing cloud infrastructure..."
- Avoid excessive reconnect framing like "Hope it's been a great year since we last spoke".

**Implication & Insight (Challenge):**
- Introduce a challenge relevant to the recipient based on the observation. 
- Example: "...Managing costs during this kind of transition is often a major hurdle."

**Project Experience (Proof):**
- Provide ONE strong proof point as the centerpiece of the email.
- Use the best matching project or technical asset that relates to their current challenge.
- If available, use 2-3 concise bullets to show supporting outcomes.

**Conversational Question (CTA):**
- The CTA should be a low friction re-engagement.
- Examples: "Would be great to catch up and compare notes on this.", "If relevant, happy to discuss our approach."
