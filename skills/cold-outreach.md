# Cold Outreach

**Goal:**
Introduce WhileOne and establish credibility without assuming any prior relationship.

**Tone:**
Professional, warm, and concise.

**Instructions:**
- Do not assume prior relationship or knowledge of WhileOne.
- Briefly introduce yourself and WhileOne.
- Mention relevant expertise and connect it to their industry or role.
- Avoid aggressive sales language.
- End with a soft call to action for a brief conversation.

**Structure:**
1. Introduction
2. Relevant expertise & context
3. Why we are reaching out
4. Soft CTA
