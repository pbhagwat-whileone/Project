# Unknown Strategy

**Goal:**
Establish contact in a professional and broadly applicable manner when the relationship is unknown or unspecified.

**Tone:**
Professional, warm, and concise.

**Instructions:**
- Keep it formal but warm.
- Provide brief context about who you are.
- Connect your expertise broadly to their industry.
- Use a soft, lower-friction call to action.

**Structure:**
1. Professional greeting
2. Brief introduction
3. Relevant expertise
4. Soft CTA
