# Mutual Connection

**Goal:**
Establish contact by leveraging a shared professional or personal connection.

**Tone:**
Warm, professional, and respectful.

**Instructions:**
- Mention the mutual connection immediately to establish trust.
- Explain briefly how you know the mutual connection or why they suggested reaching out.
- Tie the introduction into a relevant business context or shared interest.
- Suggest a brief, low-friction conversation.

**Structure:**
1. Mention the mutual connection
2. Context of the introduction
3. Relevant business context
4. Low-friction CTA
